*people you know* is an app for adults. This page is the standard it holds
against child sexual abuse and exploitation, which Google Play requires every
app in its social category to publish. Ours is short, because most of what
such a standard usually has to describe cannot happen here by construction.

**Last updated:** 26 September 2026.

## The rule

Child sexual abuse material is forbidden here, as is any sexualisation of a
minor, and so is using the app to contact, groom or solicit a child. This is
not a matter of degree and there is no warning: an account that does any of
it is deleted rather than suspended, and reported.

## Nobody under eighteen

The app is for people aged eighteen and over. Signing up asks for a date of
birth before it asks for an address—deliberately in that order, so that
somebody turned away at the door has not handed over a verified address for
nothing—and an account that does not attest to eighteen is never created. We
keep the moment the gate was passed and not the date that was typed.

## There is no way in from outside

Most child safety work on a social app is about strangers reaching children.
This app has no mechanism by which a stranger reaches anybody:

- There are no public profiles, no directory, and no search by name. Somebody
  can find you only by typing your exact handle or email address.
- By default, only somebody who already shares a mutual connection with you
  can ask to connect at all, and you still have to accept.
- Every connection is mutual, both people agree to it, and you can have a
  hundred and twenty-eight at most. There are no followers and no audience to
  build.
- Nothing is recommended, suggested, ranked or inserted into your feed. There
  is no surface on which an unknown adult can appear in front of anybody.

## What we can and cannot see

The app is end to end encrypted. Photographs, captions, comments and names are
encrypted on the phone that made them, with keys we never see. We therefore
do not scan content, filter it, or review it proactively, and we cannot: there
is nothing for a scanner to read. Our [moderation policy](/moderation/) says
the same at greater length.

We say this plainly rather than implying a capability we do not have. What it
means in practice is that our child safety work is enforcement on reports and
on accounts, and prevention by design, rather than detection in content.

## Reporting

**In the app.** **report** is under **more** on a post, on a person's profile,
and on a request somebody sends you. A comment has no **report** of its own, so
report the post it is under or the person who wrote it. The report says what it points at and which account that belongs to, and what
you write in the box reaches us in the clear, unlike everything else. The
person reported is never told that a report exists, or who filed it.

**By email**, if it needs a person rather than a button, or if you are not a
user of the app: pykapp+safety@proton.me.

Because we never receive a copy of the content, please say in words what is
wrong. It is the one part of a report we can read.

## What happens then

An account we find to be engaged in child sexual abuse or exploitation is
deleted, which severs every connection it has and removes its posts and
comments for everybody at once. We preserve the account records we hold when
we do, and we report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children
(NCMEC) and to law enforcement in the jurisdictions that apply to us. We
comply with lawful requests from those authorities.
