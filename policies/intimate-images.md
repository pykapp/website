If an intimate photo or video of you is on *people you know* and you did not
agree to it being shared, we will remove it. You do not need an account to
ask, and somebody can ask for you.

**Last updated:** 30 September 2026.

## What counts

A photo or video that shows a person's private parts or sexual activity,
including a fake made to look real, shared without that person's consent.

## How to ask

Email pykapp+safety@proton.me with these four things:

1. **Your name**, typed at the end of the email. That is your signature. If
   you are asking for somebody else, give your name and theirs, and say that
   you are acting for them.
2. **Enough for us to find it**: the handle of the account that shared it,
   about when it was shared, and anything else you know about the post, such
   as its caption or who was sent it.
3. **A sentence saying you believe in good faith** that it was shared without
   the consent of the person shown.
4. **How to reach you**, if not by replying to your email.

## What we do

We remove it within 48 hours of getting a request with those four things.
Removing a post takes it away from everyone it was shared with, and we also
remove every reshare of it.

If what you sent is not enough for us to find it, we write back and ask for
more.

If the person shown is under 18, say so. We also report it, as our [child
safety standards](/child-safety/) describe.

## What we cannot do

Everything on *people you know* is end-to-end encrypted. We cannot see the
photo, search for it, or recognise another copy of it posted separately. That
is why we need the account and about when.

Anyone the photo was shared with can also report the post from the app. That
points us at the exact post.
