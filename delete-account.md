This page explains how to delete your *people you know* account. You can
delete it in the app. If you cannot get into the app, because you deleted the
app, lost your phone or can no longer read your email, [there are other
ways](#if-you-cannot-get-into-the-app), including asking us by email, even if
you can no longer use the email address on the account.

**Last updated:** 10 November 2026.

If you only want to delete a post, a comment or your profile picture, [that is
further down](#deleting-a-post-a-comment-or-your-profile-picture), and you do
not need to write to us.

## If you pay for a subscription

- **Google Play:** if you have not cancelled your subscription, deleting your
  account cancels it, so Google Play stops charging you for it. Deleting does
  not refund what you have already paid. If you ask us by email to delete the
  account, the subscription is cancelled when we delete the account, and it
  goes on renewing until then. To stop it sooner, cancel it in your
  [subscriptions on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).
- **App Store:** deleting your account does not cancel your subscription, and
  Apple does not let us cancel it for you. If you have not cancelled it, Apple
  keeps charging you. You can cancel it before or after you delete the
  account, in your
  [subscriptions in the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions),
  from an iPhone, iPad or Mac. From a Windows computer,
  [Apple's help page](https://support.apple.com/118428) explains how.

## In the app

To keep a copy of your posts, with their photos and videos, export your data
before you delete anything: *settings → export my data*.

Then *settings → delete my account*. The app asks you to confirm once, and
then the account is deleted immediately. You cannot undo it. [What deleting
does](#what-deleting-does) is below. Deleting the app from your phone does not
delete the account.

Check where the exported file is before you delete the app itself. On Android
it is in Downloads, and it stays when the app is deleted. On an iPhone it is
in the Files app, in *On My iPhone → people you know*, unless you moved it.
Deleting your account does not touch it, but deleting the app deletes that
folder with the file in it. Move the file out of that folder first, to iCloud
Drive for example. If you already deleted the app on an iPhone, a file left in
that folder was deleted with it.

## If you cannot get into the app

If you deleted the app or lost your phone, but you can still sign in and you
have your recovery phrase, the six words the app gave you to write down, you
can install the app on any phone, sign in, export your data if you want it,
and delete the account there at once. Without the phrase, a new phone cannot
open the account, so write to us instead.

To ask us, write to **pykapp+privacy@proton.me** from the email address on the
account, asking us to delete it and giving your handle. Your sign-in codes go
to that address, so a message from it shows us that the account is yours. We
delete the account within 30 days and write back when it is done. Until then,
the account and your posts stay as they are.

If the account has no email address, or you can no longer read the one it
has, write from any address and give your handle. We will ask you for
something only that account's owner could know, and we will not delete the
account without it: a wait of a day or two is better for you than an account
anybody could delete just by giving its handle. Never send your recovery
phrase to anybody, us included.

## What deleting does

- Your posts, comments and reactions disappear for everyone straight away.
- You can no longer see what other people shared with you: your keys to their
  posts are destroyed at once. Their posts stay for everybody else.
- Your name and profile picture are deleted. The picture's file leaves our
  storage within the hour, and our backup copy a little over 30 days later.
- You are no longer connected to anybody. The groups you made are deleted, and
  you are taken out of other people's.
- Albums you made keep only what others posted in them, and nobody can add to
  them any more.
- Every phone you were signed in on is signed out.
- Anybody can take your handle, and the email address or phone number on the
  account can be used for a new one.
- If you used a password to sign in, what we kept to check it is deleted. We
  never had the password itself.

Your photos and videos are stored on our servers as encrypted files. After the
account is deleted, they are removed in two steps:

- from our storage, eight days later;
- from our backup copy of those files, a little over 30 days after the first
  step.

So the last of the files is gone within six weeks of the account being deleted.
When you delete in the app, it names the day by which both steps are done,
just under six weeks away.

Those weeks are not a chance to undo. The keys that opened the files were
destroyed when the account was deleted, and nothing in the app can bring them
back. If you sign in again with the same address, even during those weeks, you
make a new, empty account.

Backups of our database are kept 35 days, so the account as it was before you
deleted it can stay in them that long. It can also stay for up to a week in
extracts of the database we made ourselves. We never bring a deleted account
back from a backup: if we ever restore the database from one, every account
deleted since that backup was made is deleted again before anybody can use the
app.

Some records stay after the account is deleted, kept under a number that no
longer leads to your name, handle, email address or keys. They include that
the account existed; blocks and reports, by you or about you; the country set
in your phone's settings; the add requests you sent and received; when you
signed in; and a record of each of your posts and comments, saying when it was
made and, for a post, who it was shared with. The [privacy policy](/privacy/)
lists them all. The text of your comments stays too, encrypted and shown to
nobody, for as long as the posts they are on exist.

## Deleting a post, a comment or your profile picture

Each of these is done in the app, and takes effect the moment you tap it.

- A post of yours: *more → delete for everyone*. Everybody loses it and its
  comments at once, you included, and it cannot be brought back: the keys that
  opened it are destroyed. Its encrypted files are removed from our storage
  eight days later and from our backup copy a little over 30 days after that,
  as they are when an account is deleted. The app names the day when you
  delete.
- A comment: *more → delete*, on your own comment or on any comment on your
  own post. Nobody sees it any more. Its text stays on our servers, encrypted
  and unreadable to us, for as long as the post it is on exists.
- A reaction: tap it again and it is gone.
- Your name on somebody else's post: *more → remove my tag*. Your name comes
  off the post, and people who saw your name and profile picture only because
  of the tag no longer see them. The post itself stays.
- Your profile picture: tap it on your own profile, then *remove*. From that
  moment, nobody sees it any more. The file is removed from our storage
  within the hour, and from our backup copy a little over 30 days later.
- Somebody you no longer want to share with: remove or block them, with *more*
  on their profile. Neither of you sees the other's posts any more, except,
  after a removal, in albums a third person made. The
  [guide](/how-it-works/#muting-removing-and-blocking) says what each one does.

None of this can delete a copy somebody already saved to their phone, or a
screenshot. That is true of anything you share with anybody, here or
elsewhere, and no setting can change it.

The [privacy policy](/privacy/) says what we hold while you have the account.
