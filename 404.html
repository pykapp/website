--- permalink: /404.html layout: null sitemap: false --- {%- comment -%} The page GitHub Pages serves for every address with nothing at it, which makes it the page a profile link lands on when the app is not installed (DESIGN.md §6). peopleyouk.now/@ opens the app where it is installed, because the two files in .well-known/ say it may. Anywhere else the browser asks the site, no page of the site starts with /@, and this answers. The script tells the two apart by the shape of the address and nothing else: it asks no server, so this page cannot say whether anybody holds the handle, and its words never claim that they do. On an iPhone it goes on to the App Store. On Android it hands the same link to the app by its package, which Chrome opens where the app is installed; where it is not, the page is still in front two seconds later and goes on to Google Play. On a computer it stays, and says to open the link on a phone. Without the script, and for any address that is not a handle, it says nothing is here. It picks English or French the way the app does, by the first subtag of the browser's language, and by /fr/ for a missing French page. One language is shown at a time. {%- endcomment -%} {{ site.title }}
